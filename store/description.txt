Soft color fields. Huge time. One quiet caption.

Wash turns your Pebble Time 2 or Round 2 into a tiny gallery piece: overlapping rounded color slabs fill the screen, and the time sits large and calm on top. No weather clutter. No dashboard chrome. Just atmosphere you can customize.

PALETTES
Harbor, Blush, Night Bloom, Paper, Ochre, Fog, Acid, Ink — or pick your own background, three wash colors, and time color.

COMPOSITION
Cascade, Center bloom, Edge hug, Editorial, or Auto (the layout reflows through the day). Choose 2–4 fields and soft halo edges or hard cuts.

TIME
Display, classic, or compact numerals. Center, lower-left editorial, or upper banner. Caption can be date, weekday, battery, steps, or off.

BATTERY
Updates once a minute. No continuous seconds. No live heart-rate sampling. At 15% battery, washes drop away and only time + battery remain.

Open Wash settings in the Pebble app to make it yours.
